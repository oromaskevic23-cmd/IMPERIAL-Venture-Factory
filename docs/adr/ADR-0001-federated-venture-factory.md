# ADR-0001 — Federated Multi-Orchestrator Venture Factory

**Status:** Proposed; Founder approval required  
**Date:** 2026-10-26

## Decision
Use a federated set of bounded orchestration domains for Discovery, Engineering, Assurance, Commercial Operations and Exit. ASTRA coordinates intent and milestones but does not function as an unrestricted global operator.

## Why
Different ventures must remain independently deployable, sellable, exportable and separately authorized. Shared root-level credentials would create single points of security and asset-transfer failure. Federated authority aligns with zero-trust boundaries and separation of duties.

## Consequences
- Each venture receives its own ID, repository/data/cost boundaries, scoped NCA identities, approved Enterprise Skills and auditable permits.
- Sensitive actions route through Approval Gateway with Guardian Core/CYBER HAK checks, ProofGraph and Audit Ledger.
- Technology IP transfer must be explicit and separately documented.
- Development may use shared engineering tools but must not require transferring strategic IMPERIAL Core infrastructure to an acquirer.
- Operational complexity increases; templates and automated offline gate validation reduce drift.

## Rejected alternative
One always-on super-orchestrator with global admin rights and automatic authority to deploy, spend, publish, sell or move credentials — rejected as unsafe and incompatible with final Architect authority.
