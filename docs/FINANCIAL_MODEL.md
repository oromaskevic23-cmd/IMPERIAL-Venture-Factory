# Finance Model — Evidence-First, No Guaranteed Returns

The ten programs are proposed assets and **have no verified new-product revenue** at foundation stage.

## Definitions
- `MRR`: monthly recurring invoiced subscription revenue, excluding one-time fees. Distinguish booked, billed and cash-collected.
- `Gross margin`: collected revenue minus direct delivery costs; document hosting, model inference, storage, payment and support expenses.
- `Owner-adjusted earnings`: profit after normalizing work done by owner; disclose methodology.
- `ARR`: recurring MRR × 12 when subscriptions genuinely recur.
- `Churn`: canceled customers or revenue divided by corresponding starting cohort; disclose exclusions.

## Valuation example ONLY (illustrative)
20 customers × $39 /month = **$780 MRR**; annualized revenue = **$9,360**.
At **75% assumed** profit margin, annual profit = **$7,020**.
At **3.9× assumed** profit multiple, illustrative asset value = **$27,378**.
Ten ventures all meeting identical *assumptions* = **$273,780**, not a price estimate or promise. Confirm actual customers, cost structure, buyer terms and market comps prior to quoting values.
